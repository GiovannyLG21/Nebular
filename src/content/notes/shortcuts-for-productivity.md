---
title: "Shortcuts for Productivity"
date: "2024-10-04T10:45"
---

Learning keyboard shortcuts can greatly improve your workflow. For example, using `Ctrl+C` and `Ctrl+V` for copy and paste, `Alt+Tab` to switch between applications, or `Cmd+Shift+P` in VS Code to access commands. Master a few at a time and watch your productivity soar!
