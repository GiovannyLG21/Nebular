# Basics of SEO

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) helps make your site more visible in search results. Focus on both technical and content-related improvements.

# Checklist

- Optimize title tags and meta descriptions
- Use header tags (H1, H2…)
- Build meaningful backlinks
- Ensure fast load times

Improving SEO brings more organic visitors to your site.
