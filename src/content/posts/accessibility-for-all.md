# Why Accessibility Matters

An accessible website is usable by people with a wide range of abilities and disabilities. It's an ethical obligation and often a legal requirement.

# Key Tips

- Use semantic HTML
- Ensure sufficient color contrast
- Provide alt text for images
- Make sure your site is keyboard navigable

Accessibility isn’t a one-time fix—it’s an ongoing commitment in your development process.
